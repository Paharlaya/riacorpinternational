Dual-Gateway Supply Chain & Logistics

Two operating corridors, each matched to what it carries.

The East Route

Himalayan Botanicals Corridor (Kolkata Port)

A high-altitude sourcing and processing network based around Kathmandu, Nepal. Cargo is secured from mountain growing regions, coordinated regionally through the Siliguri/Darjeeling corridor, and exported through the Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port in Kolkata where applicable.

The West & South Route

Industrial Bio-Nutrient Clusters (Western & Southern Ports)

Specialized manufacturing networks across India's bio-industrial zones in Western and Southern India produce our technical bio-nutrients, dietary fibres and active botanical extracts. Shipments clear and dispatch through the most suitable verified deepwater gateway, including Mundra, Nhava Sheva (JNPT) or Chennai where applicable.

Routing Corridors describe how we normally operate. The final route for any order depends on origin, destination, availability and shipping conditions, and is confirmed at quotation.
