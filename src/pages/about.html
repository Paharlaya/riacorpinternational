The Corporate Vision

Riacorp International is a merchant exporter operating at the intersection of natural sourcing and industrial quality. We supply international B2B buyers through direct sourcing from verified growers and audited Indian/Himalayan partner manufacturing facilities, placing material with buyers who purchase against a written specification.

Our operational and export coordination base is in Siliguri and Darjeeling, West Bengal, India — on the doorstep of the Himalayan growing regions and the Kolkata export corridor.

Trade & logistics management

Sourcing & technical network

Verified growers and partner facilities
