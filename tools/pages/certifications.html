Certifications

Fully licensed. Internationally compliant.

Every registration required to export food and agricultural products legally from India, plus the food-safety and religious certifications your market asks for. Copies are supplied on request.

Indian export registrations

The statutory set. Without these an Indian exporter cannot legally ship food or agricultural goods.

DGFT, Ministry of Commerce

Import Export Code (IEC)

The ten-digit code that authorises a business to import and export from India. Quoted on every shipping bill and customs declaration.

APEDA

APEDA Registration

Agricultural & Processed Food Products Export Development Authority registration, required for exporting scheduled agri-commodities.

FSSAI

FSSAI Licence

Food Safety and Standards Authority of India licence confirming that processing, handling and packing meet Indian food safety law.

Government of India

GST Registration

Valid GSTIN under the Goods & Services Tax framework, so invoices and refunds stand up to audit in both countries.

Spices Board India

Spices Board RCMC

Registration Cum Membership Certificate as an exporter of spices, required for spice consignments leaving India.

DGFT

2-Star Export House

Status recognition granted on export performance, giving faster customs handling and procedural concessions.

Ministry of MSME, India

Udyam Registration

Government registration of the enterprise under the MSME framework, with a verifiable Udyam number.

State factory inspectorate

Factory Licence

Licence authorising manufacturing at the processing premises, covering safety, labour and operating conditions.

Food safety and quality systems

Audited standards held at the processing units our goods pass through.

BRCGS

BRC Global Standard

Global food safety standard audited on site, and the one most European and UK retailers treat as the entry ticket.

ISO

ISO 22000:2018

Food safety management system covering hazard control across the whole chain, from intake to despatch.

Codex Alimentarius

HACCP

Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points plan with documented monitoring at each critical control point.

Good Manufacturing Practice

GMP

Documented manufacturing, hygiene and record-keeping practice across processing and packing.

US Food & Drug Administration

USFDA Facility Registration

Food facility registration with the FDA, with prior notice filed on every consignment entering the United States.

FDA, FSMA

FSMA & FSVP

Foreign Supplier Verification Programme documentation for US importers, under the Food Safety Modernization Act.

Organic, Kosher and Halal

Issued per consignment where your market or customer requires it.

NPOP, APEDA

India Organic (NPOP)

National Programme for Organic Production certification with traceability back to the certified farm group.

USDA

USDA Organic (NOP)

National Organic Program certification for organic psyllium and spices entering the United States.

EC 2018/848

EU Organic

European organic certification, with the control body code and certificate of inspection issued per shipment.

Orthodox Union

Kosher (OU)

Kosher certification for the processing lines, with the OU mark permitted on retail packs.

Recognised Halal authority

Halal

Halal certification covering processing and packing. Plant-based goods are inherently Halal; the formal certificate is issued on request.

Product documentation

Material Safety Data Sheet

MSDS and technical data sheet issued per product for customs, warehousing and downstream handling.
