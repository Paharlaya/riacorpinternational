Before you write

The more of this you send first time, the faster the quote comes back — usually the same day rather than the third email.

For spices

Name the grade you want, not just the spice: Teja S17 or Byadgi chilli, Singapore 99% or Europe 99.5% cumin, mesh size for anything ground.

For psyllium

Tell us purity (98% or 99%), mesh if you want powder, whether it must be certified organic, and the swell volume your formulation needs.

For every enquiry

Destination port, target shipment month, packing size and whether you need private labelling. Incoterm too, if your forwarder has told you.
