How a shipment moves with us

Six steps from your first enquiry to a container on the water. You get a named contact for all of them.

  1. Enquiry and quotation

    Send product, grade, quantity, destination port and the Incoterm you work on. A proforma invoice follows within 24 to 48 hours.

  2. Sample approval

    We courier a sample of the exact lot grade. The bulk shipment matches what you approved — that is the whole point of the step.

  3. Order confirmation

    Confirm against the proforma, open the LC or release the advance. Procurement starts the same day.

  4. Processing and testing

    Cleaning, grading, sortex, milling where applicable, then lab testing at an NABL-accredited facility.

  5. Packing and inspection

    Packed to your specification, private-labelled if required, and offered for third-party inspection before the container is sealed.

  6. Documents and despatch

    Bill of lading, certificate of origin, phytosanitary and health certificates issued, and container tracking shared with you.

Why buyers stay with us

Agricultural exporting is an industry where the second order matters more than the first. Anyone can send a good sample. What buyers come back for is the container that matches it.

So we keep the chain short, test every lot rather than every consignment, and put the quality inspection report in the document set instead of waiting to be asked for it. If something is wrong, we would rather tell you before the vessel sails than argue about it after discharge.
