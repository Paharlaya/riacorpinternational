Quality proven by evidence, not badges.

How material is qualified, traced, tested and documented before it reaches you — and the registrations that sit behind it. Certificate copies are supplied to qualified buyers on request.

How quality is controlled

Eight controls, each producing a record. Ask for any of them against a specific lot.

Supplier & facility qualification

Growing regions, processing units and manufacturing facilities are assessed before any material is contracted, covering capability, hygiene practice and the certifications each site holds.

Raw-material & batch traceability

Every consignment carries a lot reference traceable back through processing to the sourcing region, so a query about one bag resolves against one record.

Sampling & laboratory testing

Representative samples are drawn from the production lot and tested against the agreed specification. Retention samples are held for the shelf life of the material.

Certificate of Analysis workflow

A COA is issued per lot, not per shipment, and states the grade actually supplied. Pre-shipment COAs are available for review before goods are released.

Contaminant & microbiological testing

Heavy metals, microbiological limits and pesticide residue are tested where the product and destination market require it. Applicable parameters are agreed at order stage.

Packaging & labelling controls

Fill weight, liner type, batch coding and label content are fixed against the order. Destination-specific regulatory text is checked before printing.

Export & phytosanitary documentation

Certificate of origin, phytosanitary and health certificates and customs paperwork are prepared as part of the order rather than requested afterwards.

Document availability by product & destination

Required documents differ by material and importing country. Tell us the destination with your enquiry and we will confirm the exact set before you contract.

Export registrations
