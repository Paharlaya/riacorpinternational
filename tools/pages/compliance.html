Every registration required to export food and agricultural raw materials legally, plus the audited food-safety standards your quality team will ask to see. Copies supplied on request.

Export registrations

The statutory set. Without these a consignment cannot legally leave for an international buyer.

DGFT, Ministry of Commerce

Import Export Code (IEC)

The code authorising the enterprise to import and export, quoted on every shipping bill and customs declaration.

APEDA

APEDA Registration

Agricultural & Processed Food Products Export Development Authority registration for scheduled agri-commodities.

FSSAI

FSSAI Licence

Confirms that processing, handling and packing meet statutory food safety law.

Government of India

GST Registration

Valid GSTIN so invoices and refunds stand up to audit in both jurisdictions.

Spices Board India

Spices Board RCMC

Registration Cum Membership Certificate as an exporter of spices and botanicals.

DGFT

Star Export House

Status recognition granted on export performance, giving faster customs handling and procedural concessions.

Food safety & quality systems

Audited standards held at the processing units our material passes through.

BRCGS

BRC Global Standard

Site-audited food safety standard, treated as the entry requirement by most European and UK buyers.

ISO

ISO 22000:2018

Food safety management across the whole chain, from intake to despatch.

Codex Alimentarius

HACCP

Hazard analysis with documented monitoring at each critical control point.

Good Manufacturing Practice

GMP

Documented manufacturing, hygiene and record-keeping practice across processing and packing.

US Food & Drug Administration

USFDA Facility Registration

Food facility registration with prior notice filed on consignments entering the United States.

FDA, FSMA

FSMA & FSVP

Foreign Supplier Verification Programme documentation for US importers.

Organic, Kosher & Halal

Issued per consignment where the destination market or your customer requires it.

NPOP

India Organic (NPOP)

National Programme for Organic Production certification, traceable to the certified farm group.

USDA

USDA Organic (NOP)

National Organic Program certification for organic material entering the United States.

EC 2018/848

EU Organic

European organic certification, with the control body code and certificate of inspection per shipment.

Orthodox Union

Kosher (OU)

Kosher certification covering the processing lines, with the mark permitted on retail packs.

Recognised Halal authority

Halal

Halal certification covering processing and packing, issued on request.

Product documentation

MSDS & TDS

Material safety and technical data sheets issued per product for customs and downstream handling.
