Indian Spices

Flagship

Whole, crushed and ground. Sortex-cleaned, steam-sterilised on request.

We supply the full Indian spice basket from the growing belts of Gujarat, Rajasthan, Andhra Pradesh and Kerala. Whole spices are cleaned and graded to your specification; ground spices are milled and sieved to the mesh you nominate.

Typical goods

  • Turmeric finger, turmeric powder
  • Red chilli whole, crushed, powder
  • Cumin, coriander, fennel, fenugreek, mustard, ajwain
  • Black pepper, cardamom, clove, cinnamon, bay leaf, nutmeg, star anise
  • Dehydrated onion and garlic, flakes and powder

Quality and paperwork

  • Lot-wise certificate of analysis from an NABL-accredited laboratory
  • Phytosanitary certificate and certificate of origin with every shipment
  • Steam sterilisation and ETO-free processing where the market requires it

Psyllium Husk

High purity

85% to 99% purity husk, powder from 40 to 100 mesh, seed and industrial grades.

Psyllium (Plantago ovata, isabgol) is grown in north Gujarat and western Rajasthan and milled around Unjha. We supply food, pharmaceutical and industrial grades, conventional and certified organic.

Typical goods

  • Psyllium husk 85% / 95% / 98% / 99% purity
  • Psyllium husk powder, 40 / 60 / 80 / 100 mesh
  • Organic psyllium husk and organic husk powder
  • Psyllium seed
  • Industrial Kha-Kha powder for animal feed and construction

Quality and paperwork

  • Swell volume, purity, moisture and ash reported per lot
  • Organic chain of custody under NPOP, USDA NOP and EU equivalence
  • Kosher and Halal certificates available on request
