Our story

RiaCorp International was set up to do one thing properly: get India's agricultural produce to an overseas buyer in the condition it left the processing unit, with the paperwork that lets it clear customs without an argument.

That sounds unremarkable until you have imported from India. The common failure is not fraud, it is drift — a good first sample, then a container that is half a grade down, a certificate that arrives a week after the vessel, a moisture reading nobody mentioned. Each is small. Together they cost an importer more than the price difference they were chasing.

So we built the business the other way round. We buy where the crop is grown and milled rather than through a chain of traders, because every hand the goods pass through is a place quality can slip. We test lot by lot rather than shipment by shipment, because a shipment is not a homogeneous thing. And we prepare the document set as part of the order, not as an afterthought once the container is gone.

Today that means two lines in full production — Indian spices and psyllium husk — with more coming as the volume justifies doing them to the same standard. We would rather be trusted on two products than adequate on twenty.

How we got here

Foundation

Company incorporated in India with agricultural commodity export as its purpose, and the first supplier relationships built in the Gujarat psyllium belt.

Licensing

Import Export Code, APEDA registration, FSSAI licence and Spices Board registration obtained — the statutory set for shipping food out of India.

Product focus

Two lines taken to full production: the Indian spice basket, and psyllium husk in food, pharmaceutical and industrial grades.

Why buyers choose us

Six things we do differently, and each one is checkable.

Direct farm-to-export chain

We source from verified growers and licensed processing units rather than through intermediaries. Better control of what goes in the bag, and a keener price at the same quality.

Multi-stage quality control

Graded, sortex-cleaned, lab-tested and inspected before despatch against your specification and international food safety standards.

Complete documentation

Certificate of origin, phytosanitary and health certificates, bill of lading, certificate of analysis and customs paperwork prepared as part of the order.

Flexible Incoterms and packing

EXW, FOB, CFR and CIF, with packing from 100 g retail pouches to 50 kg bulk bags and private labelling where you want it.

Samples before you commit

Free samples across every grade so your QA team can verify quality before any money moves.

One named contact

You deal with the person responsible for your shipment, who answers directly and is accountable when something goes wrong.
