How we control quality

Every step below happens before the container is sealed, and the report goes into your document set.

  1. Sourcing

    Bought at the mandi or direct from the processing unit, against the grade agreed on your order.

  2. Cleaning and grading

    De-stoned, de-stemmed and sortex-cleaned. Ground lines are milled and sieved to nominated mesh.

  3. Laboratory testing

    Moisture, ash, purity, ASTA colour, aflatoxin and pesticide residue at an NABL-accredited laboratory.

  4. Pre-shipment inspection

    Third-party inspection by SGS, Intertek, Bureau Veritas or your own agent, arranged on request.

  5. Packing under supervision

    Weighed, sealed and batch-coded, with retention samples held against the lot.

  6. Documentation

    Certificate of analysis, phytosanitary certificate, certificate of origin and health certificate issued.
