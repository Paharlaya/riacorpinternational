Buyer FAQ

The questions importers actually ask.

Ordering, samples, payment, shipping, documents and quality — answered plainly. If yours is not here, ask and we will add it.

Frequently asked questions

MOQ and ordering

What is your minimum order quantity?

Our standard MOQ is one 20 ft container, which works out at roughly 18–25 MT for spices and 12–16 MT for compressed psyllium husk depending on the grade and pack size.

Smaller trial quantities are available to first-time buyers, usually as an LCL consignment. Ask and we will quote it.

Can I combine products in one container?

Yes. Dry goods consolidate freely, so spices and psyllium can share a container. We will work out the load plan so the weight and cube both come out right.

Strongly aromatic spices are kept separated from psyllium by liner and stow position, because husk picks up odour.

How do I place an order?
  • Enquiry — product, grade, quantity, destination port, Incoterm
  • Quotation — proforma invoice within 24–48 hours
  • Sample — optional, and we recommend it for a first order
  • Confirm and pay — against the proforma, by LC or TT
  • Production and despatch — with container tracking shared
Do you publish a price list?

No. Agricultural prices move with crop, season and currency, so a published list would be wrong within a fortnight. We issue a personalised proforma invoice within 24–48 hours of your enquiry, valid for a stated period.

Do you work with first-time buyers?

Yes, and we would rather start small. Samples, a trial LCL consignment and flexible terms on a first order are all normal here. Every long-standing buyer was a first-time buyer once.

Sampling

Are samples available before a bulk order?

Yes, across every grade on both product lines. We strongly encourage it. Approving a sample is what makes the rest of the relationship straightforward.

What do samples cost?

The sample itself is free. You cover the international courier charge, typically USD 30–80 by DHL or FedEx depending on destination. If you have your own courier account we will ship on that instead.

How long does a sample take to arrive?

Despatched within 2–3 working days. Transit is roughly 3–5 days to the Middle East, 4–7 days to Europe, 5–8 days to Africa and 5–10 days elsewhere.

Will the bulk order match the sample?

Yes, and this is the commitment the whole business rests on. The sample is drawn from the same grade and specification that will be supplied in bulk, and a Certificate of Analysis accompanies every shipment so you can check it against what you approved.

We also hold a retention sample against each lot for the shelf life of the product.

Payment terms

What payment methods do you accept?
  • LC — Letter of Credit, at sight or usance
  • TT — telegraphic transfer, typically 30% advance and 70% against a copy of the bill of lading
  • DP — Documents against Payment
  • DA — Documents against Acceptance, for established buyers
Which currency do you invoice in?

USD by default, as the standard currency for agricultural commodity trade. EUR and AED can be arranged where it suits your banking.

Can I open a Letter of Credit?

Yes, from any internationally recognised bank. We ask that the LC is irrevocable, allows partial shipment and transhipment, and carries a tolerance of plus or minus 5% on quantity and value — agricultural goods rarely load to the exact kilo.

Is there volume pricing?

Yes, above roughly 100 MT per product, and for buyers placing regular monthly or quarterly orders. Repeat business earns better terms than a single large order.

Lead time and shipping

How long from order confirmation to shipment?
  • Spices, whole or ground — 10–15 working days
  • Psyllium husk and powder — 15–20 working days
  • Organic grades — add 5–7 days for transaction certification
  • Custom printed packaging — add 7–10 days
Which ports do you ship from?

Mundra and Kandla in Gujarat, which suit psyllium; Nhava Sheva for general cargo; Chennai and Cochin for southern spices. We pick the port that gets your cargo on the best rotation, not the one closest to us.

How long is ocean transit?
  • Gulf states — 7–12 days
  • Southeast Asia — 10–16 days
  • East Africa — 14–20 days
  • Europe and West Africa — 20–28 days
  • North America — 22–30 days
  • Australia and New Zealand — 18–25 days
Which Incoterms do you offer?

EXW, FOB, CFR/CNF and CIF. FOB and CIF cover most of what we do. Tell us which your freight forwarder prefers and we will quote on it.

Do you provide shipment tracking?

Yes. Once the vessel sails you get the bill of lading with vessel and voyage number, the container number for live tracking, and the ETA at your discharge port.

Documentation

What documents come with every shipment?

Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, phytosanitary certificate, health certificate, certificate of analysis and quality inspection report. Fumigation and insurance certificates are added where the market or Incoterm requires them.

Do you provide a phytosanitary certificate?

Yes, issued by the Plant Quarantine Authority of India and included with every agricultural shipment at no extra charge.

How is the certificate of origin issued?

By an authorised body — usually FIEO, a chamber of commerce, or the relevant Export Promotion Council. Where your country has a preferential trade agreement with India, we issue the preferential form so you can claim the duty benefit.

Can you meet my country's specific customs requirements?

Yes. Requirements differ — legalisation at the consulate for some Gulf states, a certificate of inspection for EU organic, prior notice filing for the US. Tell us the destination and we will prepare accordingly rather than send a generic set.

Certifications and compliance

What certifications do you hold?

IEC, APEDA, FSSAI, GST, Spices Board RCMC, Udyam and 2-Star Export House status on the Indian side, and BRC, ISO 22000:2018, HACCP, GMP and USFDA registration with FSMA/FSVP on the food-safety side. Organic, Kosher and Halal certificates are issued where required.

Do your products meet EU pesticide MRLs?

Yes. EU maximum residue limits are the tightest we work to, so lots destined for Europe are tested for residues at an NABL-accredited laboratory before packing, and we will not ship a lot that fails.

Are your products Halal?

Spices and psyllium are plant-based with no animal content, so they are inherently Halal. Where your customer or customs authority wants a formal certificate we arrange one from a recognised authority.

Can I arrange my own third-party inspection?

Yes, and we encourage it. We work routinely with SGS, Intertek, Bureau Veritas, COTECNA and TÜV SÜD, and the container is held until the report clears.

Quality and packaging

How do you keep quality consistent between shipments?
  • Sourcing from the same verified growers and processing units
  • Grading against a written specification, not a verbal one
  • Sortex cleaning and metal detection
  • Lot-wise testing at an NABL-accredited laboratory
  • Retention samples held against every lot
  • Certificate of analysis issued per lot, not per shipment
What packaging options are available?

Retail packs from 100 g to 1 kg, wholesale bags at 5, 10, 25 and 50 kg in PP woven, multi-wall paper or jute with a food-grade liner. Psyllium ships compressed in 20 and 25 kg bags.

Private labelling with your brand, nutritional panel, barcode and destination regulatory text is available.

What is the shelf life?
  • Whole spices — 24 months in original packing
  • Ground spices — 18–24 months
  • Psyllium husk and powder — 24 months
  • Dehydrated onion and garlic — 18–24 months

All figures assume cool, dry storage away from direct sunlight and strong odours.

What if a shipment arrives damaged or off-specification?

Notify us within 7 days of discharge with photographs and, where relevant, an independent lab report. We check it against the retention sample and the certificate of analysis for that lot.

If the claim stands up, we replace the goods on the next shipment or issue a credit note. We would far rather settle it than argue it.

Still have a question?

Ask it directly. You will get an answer from the person who would handle your shipment, within 24 hours.
