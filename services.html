Services

Three product lines, one team from source to delivery.

Spices and agro products, wellness raw materials and collagen raw materials. For each one we handle sourcing, testing, documentation and freight, and quote against the terms that suit your deal.

What we trade

Each line has its own supplier network, its own tests and its own certificates. Here is what to expect from us in each.

Agro products

Export

Spices and farm produce from smallholders and processors we know by name, cleaned, graded and packed for the destination market. Spices are our largest line; pulses, tea and dried herbs travel alongside them.

Typical goods

  • Turmeric fingers and powder, dried ginger, large cardamom and green cardamom
  • Black pepper, cumin, coriander, fenugreek, dried red chilli and cinnamon
  • Whole, ground, steam-sterilised or blended to your recipe
  • Pulses, orthodox tea and dried herbs on request

Quality and paperwork

  • Cleanliness, moisture and extraneous matter to ASTA or buyer specification
  • Batch testing for aflatoxin, pesticide residues and heavy metals against the destination's limits, not the origin's
  • Steam sterilisation and microbial testing where the buyer requires it
  • Phytosanitary certificate, health certificate, certificate of origin, fumigation certificate and batch certificate of analysis with every consignment
Retail packs or bulkOrganic where certifiedPre-shipment samples

Wellness raw materials

Export and import

Botanical powders, standardised extracts and natural ingredients for supplement, functional food and cosmetic manufacturers. We match the material to your formulation and confirm the specification before anything is produced.

Typical goods

  • Ashwagandha, moringa, amla, tulsi, shatavari, brahmi and giloy as powders or extracts
  • Turmeric extract standardised to curcumin content, and other standardised extracts to your target active
  • Essential oils, Himalayan pink salt, honey and herbal teas
  • Ingredients you specify that are not on this list, sourced and verified

Quality and paperwork

  • Certificate of analysis for every batch covering identity, assay, heavy metals and microbial limits
  • Standardisation confirmed against your specification, for example withanolide or curcumin percentage
  • GMP manufacturing, and organic, halal or kosher certification where you need it
  • Non-irradiated and residue-tested material; safety data sheet and technical data sheet supplied
Samples before orderSpecification matchedCoA per batch

Collagen raw materials

Import

Hydrolysed collagen peptides and gelatin for nutraceutical, beauty and food producers, imported from audited manufacturers and supplied to a written specification.

Typical goods

  • Bovine collagen peptides, type I and III, from hide, typically 3,000 to 5,000 Da
  • Marine collagen peptides, type I, from fish skin and scales, typically 2,000 to 3,000 Da
  • Type II collagen from chicken cartilage for joint formulations
  • Edible gelatin by bloom strength, and unflavoured or flavoured peptide blends

Quality and paperwork

  • Specification agreed on molecular weight, protein content, solubility, colour, odour and taste
  • Certificate of analysis per lot, with heavy metals, microbial and allergen results
  • Halal and kosher certificates, GMP and ISO 22000 or HACCP status of the manufacturer
  • Veterinary and health certificates, BSE-free origin statement for bovine material, and import permits handled before shipment

Behind every shipment

The same four services sit under all three product lines. Use them together or pick the ones you are missing.

Sourcing and verification

Supplier identification, factory or farm visits, sampling and price negotiation before you commit.

Quality and compliance

Laboratory testing against the destination's limits, pre-shipment inspection and labelling checks.

Documentation and customs

HS classification, permits, certificates and declarations prepared and filed correctly the first time.

Freight and delivery

Sea, air or road on the lane that fits your budget, insured, tracked and cleared at destination.

Terms we quote against

Incoterms decide who pays for what and where the risk changes hands. We quote on the terms that suit your deal and explain them in plain words.

EXWEx Works. You collect from the seller's door; we take it from there.
FOBFree on Board. Seller delivers on the vessel; buyer carries it onward.
CIFCost, Insurance and Freight to the destination port, insured.
DAPDelivered at Place. To your address, duties left to you.
DDPDelivered Duty Paid. To your address, everything settled.
