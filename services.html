Services

Everything between the order and the delivery.

Use one service or the whole chain. Every engagement starts with a written quote and a named coordinator.

Export management

For producers, workshops and traders who want to sell abroad without building an export department.

  • Buyer-ready quotations and proforma invoices
  • Product compliance and labelling for the destination market
  • Export licences, certificates of origin, phytosanitary and fumigation certificates where required
  • Letters of credit, advance payment and open-account terms handled with your bank

Import and sourcing

For businesses that need goods, machinery or materials from overseas and want a partner on the ground.

  • Supplier identification and verification, including factory visits
  • Sampling, specification sign-off and price negotiation
  • Pre-shipment inspection against the approved sample
  • Landed-cost calculation before you commit to an order

Customs and documentation

The part that stalls most shipments. We prepare and check every document before goods leave.

  • HS classification and duty estimates for both ends of the journey
  • Import and export declarations filed with the authorities
  • Permits, licences and restricted-goods handling
  • Document sets checked against the receiving country's requirements

Freight forwarding

Sea, air, road and multimodal, booked on the lane that fits your budget and your deadline.

  • Full-container and less-than-container sea freight
  • Air freight for perishable, urgent or high-value goods
  • Cargo insurance arranged per shipment
  • Tracking references, milestone updates and destination clearance

Packing, marking and warehousing

Goods arrive in the condition they left, marked so that every party along the route can read them.

  • Export-grade packing, palletising and moisture protection
  • Shipping marks and labels to the buyer's specification
  • Consolidation of several suppliers into one shipment
  • Short-term storage before dispatch

Trade advisory

Straight answers before you sign, from people who move goods every week.

  • Choosing the right Incoterms for the deal
  • Market-entry requirements and product standards
  • Payment risk and documentation review
  • Tariff and trade-agreement guidance

Terms we quote against

Incoterms decide who pays for what and where the risk changes hands. We quote on the terms that suit your deal and explain them in plain words.

EXWEx Works. You collect from the seller's door; we take it from there.
FOBFree on Board. Seller delivers on the vessel; buyer carries it onward.
CIFCost, Insurance and Freight to the destination port, insured.
DAPDelivered at Place. To your address, duties left to you.
DDPDelivered Duty Paid. To your address, everything settled.
