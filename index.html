Dual-Gateway Supply Chain & Logistics

Two independent corridors, each matched to what it carries. Neither depends on the other.

The East Route

Himalayan Botanicals Corridor (Kolkata Port)

Our premium agricultural portfolio is anchored by an exclusive, asset-integrated processing and milling network based in Kathmandu, Nepal. Cargo is secured from high-altitude terrains, routed under strict supervision through our strategic regional command transit hub in the Siliguri/Darjeeling corridor, and dispatched worldwide via the Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port in Kolkata.

The West & South Route

Industrial Bio-Nutrient Clusters (Western & Southern Ports)

Our technical bio-nutrients, dietary fibers, and active botanical extracts are manufactured in highly specialized, certified clinical facilities across India's premier bio-industrial zones in Western and Southern India. To guarantee optimal shelf-life and zero transit friction, these shipments clear customs and dispatch directly through advanced deepwater gateways, including Mundra, Nhava Sheva (JNPT), and Chennai ports.
