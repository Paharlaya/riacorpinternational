How a shipment moves with us

Six stages, one person answering for all of them. You hear from us before you need to ask.

  1. Enquiry and quote

    Tell us the goods, quantity, origin and destination. You get a landed-cost estimate and a realistic timeline, not a guess.

  2. Sourcing and sampling

    We find or verify the supplier, agree specifications and send samples for your approval before anything is produced.

  3. Quality check and packing

    Goods are inspected against the approved sample, then packed and marked for the route and the receiving country's rules.

  4. Documentation and customs

    Invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin, permits and declarations are prepared and filed correctly the first time.

  5. Freight and tracking

    Sea, air or road, booked on the lane that fits your budget and deadline. You get the tracking reference and milestone updates.

  6. Delivery and settlement

    Cleared at destination, delivered to the agreed point, and closed out with a clean set of final documents.

Why buyers and sellers stay with us

Trade goes wrong in the gaps: between the supplier and the forwarder, between the forwarder and the broker, between the broker and the buyer. Every hand-off is a chance for a missing document, a mislabelled carton or a week lost at a port.

We close those gaps by owning the whole journey. The person who quoted your shipment is the person who tracks it, and the person you call when something needs a decision.
