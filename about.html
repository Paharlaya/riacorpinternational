About

A trading company that treats paperwork as seriously as cargo.

Ria Corp International connects growers, processors and manufacturers with buyers across borders, and takes responsibility for the whole journey in between.

Who we are

Ria Corp International is an export and import company working in three product lines. We export spices and other agro products from farms and processors we know personally. We trade wellness raw materials, the botanical powders, extracts and natural ingredients that supplement and cosmetic makers build their products on. And we import collagen raw materials, the hydrolysed peptides and gelatin behind nutraceutical and beauty formulations.

The three lines have one thing in common: the buyer cannot see the goods until they arrive, so the specification, the tests and the certificates have to do the seeing for them. That is why we treat a certificate of analysis or a phytosanitary certificate with the same care as the cargo itself.

Most international trade problems are not about distance. They are about hand-offs. A supplier packs to one standard, a forwarder expects another, a customs broker asks for a certificate nobody mentioned. We built the company around a single principle: one team should own the shipment from the first quote to the final delivery, so that nothing falls between the gaps.

We are deliberately small enough that you will know the person handling your cargo by name, and organised enough that they will know your shipment's status before you ask.

How we work

Three habits that decide whether a shipment is routine or a story you tell later.

Plain dealing

Written quotes, clear terms, no surprise charges at the port. If a cost changes, you hear it from us first.

Tested before it travels

Nothing is loaded until the batch has been tested against the destination's limits and the full document set has been checked.

Long relationships

We would rather ship for you every month for years than win one order by promising a date we cannot keep.

Where we trade

Regular lanes to and from South Asia, the Middle East, East Asia, Europe and North America. Other destinations on request.

Work with us.

Whether you are shipping your first pallet of turmeric or your fiftieth tonne of collagen peptides, start with a conversation.
