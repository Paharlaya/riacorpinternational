About

Natural purity, industrial excellence.

A registered cross-border raw materials enterprise, built so that a procurement manager deals with one counterparty instead of a chain of them.

The Corporate Vision

Riacorp International is a registered cross-border raw materials enterprise. We operate at the intersection of natural purity and industrial excellence to provide B2B procurement managers with seamless contract fulfillment.

How the enterprise is organised

Two divisions, each accountable for a different half of the contract.

Division 01

Logistics & Operational Execution

Headquartered in the strategic Siliguri corridor, our trade management division directly oversees customs clearance, customs transit declarations, cross-border transit protocols, and multi-modal container transport.

Division 02

Technical Processing Advisory

We maintain strict oversight over our joint processing networks in Kathmandu for natural crops and certified extraction labs in Western and Southern India for functional bio-powders. This ensures every container matches requested mesh sizes, moisture parameters, active compound thresholds and safety parameters.

Who we supply

Institutional buyers with technical acceptance criteria, not retail volumes.

Procurement managers

Corporate buyers contracting raw materials against a written specification.

Food & beverage manufacturers

Fibre, botanical and protein inputs at production volume.

Pharmaceutical formulators

Standardised actives with lot-wise assay and residue data.

Cosmetics raw material buyers

Peptides and extracts specified by molecular weight and purity.

Operating principles

What the enterprise commits to on every contract.

Asset-integrated sourcing

Processing and milling capacity inside the network rather than brokered on the open market, so the specification is controlled where the material is made.

Lot-wise verification

Every consignment is released against its own Certificate of Analysis, not a shipment-level average.

Dual-corridor redundancy

Two independent export routes mean congestion on one gateway does not hold the other portfolio.

Complete documentation

Certificate of origin, phytosanitary, health certificate and customs paperwork prepared as part of the order.

Specification discipline

Mesh, moisture and active-compound thresholds are agreed in writing before production begins.

Single counterparty

One contract, one document set, one point of accountability across both pillars.
