The Corporate Vision

Riacorp operates at the intersection of natural sourcing and industrial quality to support dependable international B2B procurement. We work with growers, processing units and certified manufacturers to place raw materials with buyers who purchase against a written specification.

Operational coordination

Cross-border documentation, customs coordination, multimodal transport and shipment management.

Operational coordination

Trade & logistics management

Coordinated from the Siliguri corridor, our trade management function handles customs clearance, cross-border transit documentation and multimodal container transport, so a buyer deals with one counterparty across the whole movement.

Processing & technical network

Processing and technical network

We maintain technical oversight of the processing networks we work with — in Kathmandu for natural crops, and in Western and Southern India for functional bio-powders and extracts — so that mesh size, moisture, active-compound thresholds and safety parameters are agreed in writing before production and checked before despatch.

Who we supply

Institutional buyers with technical acceptance criteria, not retail volumes.

Procurement managers

Corporate buyers contracting raw materials against a written specification.

Food & beverage manufacturers

Pharmaceutical formulators

Cosmetics raw material buyers

Why buyers choose Riacorp

Responsive specification matching, documentation support, consolidated communication and export coordination.

Specification matching

We read your acceptance criteria first and confirm what is achievable before quoting, rather than quoting a grade and negotiating afterwards.

Lot-wise verification

Every consignment is released against its own Certificate of Analysis, not a shipment-level average.

Export coordination

Two working corridors, with the gateway chosen per shipment according to origin, destination and sailing availability.

Complete documentation

Certificate of origin, phytosanitary, health certificate and customs paperwork prepared as part of the order.

Specification discipline

Mesh, moisture and active-compound thresholds are agreed in writing before production begins.

Single counterparty

One contract, one document set, one point of accountability across both pillars.
